# Privacy

CompText Benchmark 0.1.x is a skill-only developer plugin. It does not operate a separate hosted service and does not intentionally collect or transmit telemetry on its own.

Benchmark artifacts remain in the execution environment chosen by the user. If ChatGPT, Codex, Remote Desktop Commander, Vercel, Hugging Face, or another external execution surface is used, that service's own privacy terms apply.

Do not place credentials or sensitive production data in benchmark fixtures. The plugin instructions explicitly avoid exposing environment secrets and unnecessary raw logs.
